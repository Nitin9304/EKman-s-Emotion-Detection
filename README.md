The emotion analysis based on Ekman's Basic Emotion Model demonstrates that Joy is the predominant emotional response among IPL fans, followed by Sadness and Anger. These findings indicate that YouTube comments capture a broad spectrum of fan emotions influenced by team performance, match outcomes, and player actions. Incorporating emotion analysis alongside sentiment analysis provides a more comprehensive understanding of audience behavior and enhances the effectiveness of social media analytics in sports..

The emotion wheel illustrates the six basic emotions proposed by Paul Ekman: Happiness, Surprise, Disgust, Sadness, Fear, and Anger. These emotions represent the fundamental emotional states that people commonly express in textual communication.

In this project, Ekman's emotion model complements traditional sentiment analysis by providing a more detailed understanding of user emotions. Instead of classifying comments only as positive, negative, or neutral, emotion analysis identifies the specific emotional response behind each comment. This enables a deeper understanding of fan reactions, engagement, and behavior toward IPL teams and match events.

Overall, incorporating Ekman's Basic Emotion Model enhances the effectiveness of social media analytics by providing fine-grained emotional insights, making it a valuable approach for analyzing user-generated content in sports sentiment analysis.
